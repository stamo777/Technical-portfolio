# Mechanical Engineering Technical Portfolio

This repository contains a selection of personal engineering projects focused on numerical simulation, computational mechanics, CAD and CFD.

## Projects

### Finite Element Analysis – L-Bracket

Static structural FEA study comparing an L-bracket with and without a reinforcing stiffener.

The project includes mesh convergence analysis, displacement and Von Mises stress assessment, and evaluation of the influence of the stiffener on structural stiffness.

**Tools:** FreeCAD FEM | CalculiX

➡️ [View FEA Project](FEA/)

---

### Formula 1 CAD & CFD Analysis – DRS Rear Wing

CAD modelling and CFD investigation of a simplified Formula 1 rear wing with open and closed DRS configurations.

The project includes CAD modelling, mesh generation and refinement, RANS CFD simulations, near-wall treatment, aerodynamic force calculation, and comparison of drag and downforce.

**Tools:** 3DEXPERIENCE | OpenFOAM | ParaView | ImageJ
